Insider Trading Radar

When the people inside start buying

It reads every Form 4 on EDGAR, keeps only genuine open-market purchases, and fires when three or more distinct insiders buy the same company inside a rolling window.

  • Cluster buys, weighted by role, so a CEO or CFO counts for more.
  • Standalone large buys, and first-ever CEO or CFO purchases.
  • Conviction and first-time-buyer flags on every name.
  • A new insider joining a known cluster sends an upgrade alert.
FDA Radar Alerts

Before the date that moves the stock

It keeps a live calendar of biotech catalysts, from PDUFA decisions to advisory committees and trial readouts, reminds you ahead of each one, and pings the instant news breaks.

  • Reminders at 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before a catalyst.
  • Instant alerts on approvals, CRLs and topline results.
  • Trial status and completion-date changes as they post.
  • Overdue flags when a decision date passes in silence.
